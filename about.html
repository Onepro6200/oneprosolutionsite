(01) — Who we are

One Pro Solution LLC is an international procurement and supply chain partner, headquartered in Dublin, California, with its primary hub in São Paulo, Brazil, serving buyers across Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.

Our divisions cover refined petroleum products and agricultural staples, structural construction materials, and the maritime logistics that connect them. Working through direct allocations and vetted supply chains, we deliver consistent quality on predictable schedules.

Our California trade desk is the single point of contact for every transaction — from the first ICPO to Soft Corporate Offers, allocation availability, shipping schedules and final settlement against verified documents.

(02) — Principles

How we operate

Four commitments behind every allocation we offer and every vessel we load.

Vetted supply chains

We work with qualified producers, refineries and mills through direct allocations — not speculative chains of intermediaries.

Bank-grade compliance

Transactions settle through irrevocable DLC and LC instruments from top-tier prime banks, or MT103 for approved buyers.

Independent verification

SGS or Intertek certify quality and quantity at the port of loading, so buyers pay against verified cargo.

Transparent documentation

Clear SCOs, CIF contracts under Incoterms® 2020 and a complete, compliant set of shipping documents.
