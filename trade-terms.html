CIF terms & payment instruments

(01) — Cost, Insurance & Freight

What CIF covers

The seller delivers the goods on board at the port of loading, contracts the carriage, and insures the cargo to the named destination port.

Cost

Goods supplied to contract specification, export-cleared and loaded on board.

Insurance

Marine cargo insurance placed by the seller for 110 % of the contract value.

Freight

Ocean carriage booked and paid to the named destination port.

Risk transfer

Passes to the buyer once goods are on board at the port of loading.

(03) — Transaction procedure

From ICPO to settlement

A clear sequence that protects both buyer and seller at every stage.

STEP 01

Inquiry / ICPO

The buyer submits an Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order or Letter of Intent with product specifications, quantity, destination port and proposed payment instrument.

STEP 02

Soft Corporate Offer

Our trade desk confirms allocation availability and issues an SCO setting out specifications, quantity, pricing basis, delivery schedule and terms.

STEP 03

Sale & Purchase Agreement

On acceptance of the SCO, a draft contract is issued and executed by both parties under CIF Incoterms® 2020.

STEP 04

Banking instrument

Within the agreed period, the buyer’s bank issues an irrevocable DLC or LC payable at sight, or MT103/TT where approved.

STEP 05

Inspection & loading

Cargo is inspected by SGS or Intertek at the port of loading and loaded on board; marine cargo insurance is placed.

STEP 06

Documents & settlement

Seller presents the Bill of Lading, inspection certificates and supporting documents; payment is released at sight against compliant presentation.

(05) — FAQ

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Ask the trade desk

What does CIF mean for my shipment?

Under CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight), the seller arranges and pays for carriage to the named destination port and insures the cargo during transit. Risk transfers to the buyer once the goods are on board the vessel at the port of loading, while the insurance remains in place to destination.

Which payment instruments do you accept?

Our preferred instrument is an irrevocable, confirmed Documentary Letter of Credit (DLC) payable at sight. We also accept standard commercial Letters of Credit at sight from top-tier prime banks, and MT103 / TT wire transfer for spot allocations and approved repeat institutional buyers.

Who inspects the cargo?

Quality and quantity are certified at the port of loading by SGS or Intertek. Their certificates form part of the document set presented to the bank for payment.

What do I need to submit to receive an offer?

Send your commodity and specification, quantity, destination port, delivery schedule and preferred payment instrument. Our trade desk then issues a Soft Corporate Offer (SCO).

Can you deliver to ports not listed on your routes?

Our major corridors run from our primary hub in São Paulo, Brazil, to Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas. Other destination ports can be quoted on request, subject to vessel availability and port restrictions.
