CIF Terms & Payment Instruments

All Cost, Insurance, and Freight (CIF) transactions adhere to strict international banking compliance, ensuring transparent settlement upon verified shipping documentation.

DLC — Documentary Letter of Credit (At Sight)

Irrevocable, confirmed Documentary Letter of Credit payable At Sight against presentation of compliant Bill of Lading and SGS/Intertek inspection certificates.

LC — Letter of Credit (At Sight)

Standard commercial Letter of Credit issued by top-tier prime banks with settlement strictly At Sight upon cargo loading verification.

MT103 / TT Wire Transfer

Direct bank-to-bank telegraphic transfer available for spot allocations and approved repeat institutional buyers.
