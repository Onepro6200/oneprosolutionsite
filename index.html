(01) — The Firm
op

Direct allocations and vetted supply chains, delivering institutional-grade commodities on schedule — from the refinery, the paddy field and the steel mill to your destination port.

One Pro Solution LLC is an international trade house headquartered in Dublin, California, with its primary hub in São Paulo, Brazil. We source refined petroleum products, premium Thai rice, sugar and wheat, and high-specification structural materials for commercial and civil infrastructure — and we manage the maritime logistics that bring them to market.

Every transaction is structured under CIF Incoterms® 2020, settled through irrevocable banking instruments, and certified by SGS or Intertek at the port of loading. Clear documents, verified cargo and predictable settlement for every buyer.
